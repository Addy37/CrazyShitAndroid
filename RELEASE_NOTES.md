# ZEROCHILL 3.1.0

CrazyShit for Android is now ZEROCHILL. **Install `ZeroChill.apk` over your existing app. Do not uninstall it first.** This release keeps the same Android package ID and signing certificate, so your favorites, watch history, downloads, and settings stay on your device during the update.

## What's new

- New devil-head launcher and themed icons, plus the short DEVIL WAKE startup animation.
- OLED black and electric cyan interface with updated Home, Shows, ShitTok, OnlyFap, and More navigation.
- Faster OnlyFap creator search and gallery loading, smoother gallery density changes, and media prepared ahead of your swipes.
- Consolidated OnlyFap and ShitTok notifications, refined action sheets, and improved startup and playback behavior.
- Updated companion admin app branding and public project documentation.

Account sign-in remains **Coming Soon**. Existing website sources, local lists, backups, and download behavior remain available.

Download **ZeroChill.apk** below. `CrazyShit.apk` and `ZeroFilter.apk` are byte-identical compatibility filenames for older update links. `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains the hashes for all three files. Android 8.0 or newer is required.

# CrazyShit v2.0.5

CrazyShit 2.0.5 is a small Chaos polish update focused on the scrub bar behavior.

## What's new

- The Chaos scrub bar now fades out automatically after a short period of inactivity
- Touching the video brings the scrub bar back with the rest of the playback controls
- The scrub bar stays visible while you are actively dragging it
- After you finish scrubbing, it remains visible briefly, then fades away again
- The scrub bar also auto-hides while a clip is paused, so it does not stay on screen indefinitely

## Existing Chaos improvements from 2.0.4

- Press and hold the video to temporarily play at 2x speed, then release to return to the prior speed
- Chaos remembers your mute state between clips and app sessions
- Added a More menu with Not interested, Replay, Watch Later, Comments, Share, and Open details actions
- Not interested permanently hides that clip from Chaos on the current device
- Chaos playback controls fade away automatically while a clip is playing and return with a tap
- Increased Chaos look-ahead and kept more nearby pages prepared for faster next/previous swipes
- The thin orange playback progress bar is fully scrubbable, so you can drag it left or right to seek through supported videos
- Preloaded clips do not get written to playback history unless they were actually started

## Compatibility

The stable application ID and signing identity remain unchanged, so CrazyShit v2.0.5 installs over earlier signed stable releases while retaining app data.

Beta builds use a separate `.dev` package and can remain installed beside stable CrazyShit.
